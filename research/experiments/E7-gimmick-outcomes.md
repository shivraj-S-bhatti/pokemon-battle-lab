# E7: What E2's gimmicks buy in battle

Four of E2's 45 top gimmicks (30 doubles, 15 BSS) are worth a test, two are already the standard set, and 39 are deprioritized for the selected sets and targets: on the targets checked, the receiver's selected set usually already won the matchup without the gimmick, or could not use the effect. A different set, target, move order or partner can change these verdicts.

## Top 10 by the ordering heuristic

Score = target matchups flipped per turn invested, times E2 novelty. A flip is a named target that crosses a KO threshold (native calculator `kochance`) or changes move order. Turns count the receiver's idle turns, the partner's spent actions, and a fainted partner. The score only orders this table for reading. Its denominator lumps battle turns, partner actions and sacrifices into one count, so it is a heuristic, not a measured efficiency, and it should not be used to rank gimmicks against each other outside their selected targets.

Sneasler is the only E2 top-5 entry that survives, and it survives for a narrower reason than E2 gave. The doubled Speed is not new: meta Sneasler already reaches 344 when its Psychic Seed pops under Indeedee-F. The Swagger buys only the +2 Attack, and that +2 is what turns three coin-flip KOs into guaranteed ones.

Rhyperior needs a partner that blocks Fake Out and Grassy Glide, since either kills it at 1 HP. Armor Tail Farigiraf does both and also sets Trick Room. Arcanine's result comes from the plain Arcanine usage spread, which invests 3 Attack points; a full-Attack spread already reaches some of these KOs without Beat Up, so the test should compare both spreads.

## Big effect, no outcome

**Torkoal (doubles-08), Simple Beam then Shell Smash.** Torkoal runs Quiet with 0 Speed points, so it has 36 Speed. Under Trick Room that beats all of the top 15 doubles sets. At +4 (x3) it has 108 Speed and moves after six of them in Trick Room: Incineroar (80), Indeedee-F (94), Kingambit (70), Golisopod-Mega (60), Farigiraf (80) and Milotic (101). Outside Trick Room, 108 outspeeds only those same six slow sets and nothing fast. The SpA gain changes almost nothing: +0 Eruption in sun already OHKOs Rillaboom (215-254%), Kingambit (155-184%) and Gholdengo (190-224%). The only target that flips is Farigiraf (70-83% to an OHKO), and a plain +2 Shell Smash flips it too (141-166%) without the Audino turn. The -2 Def is a real cost: Basculegion's Wave Crash goes from a 2HKO (52-63%) to an OHKO (104-124%). Net: one flip gained, six Trick Room move-order wins lost. Usage data shows no Torkoal running Shell Smash in its top moves.

The other Simple Beam entries showed limited benefit for the selected sets on the checked targets. Their accounting has three separate parts:

- **Battle turns.** Simple Beam before the ally's setup doubles that setup within one battle turn. Reaching the same stage without Simple takes the receiver two of its own setup turns.
- **Actions.** Audino spends its action on Simple Beam that turn.
- **Ability.** The receiver loses its own ability for the rest of its time on the field.

The stage reached is not always equivalent. Stockpile is capped at three uses, so plain Stockpile tops out at +3 while Simple reaches +6. For multi-stat moves the same comparison depends on how many uses the receiver can afford.

For the selected sets: Mega Blastoise's boosted Water Spout added little on the checked targets; Kommo-o without Soundproof is OHKOd by Mega Gardevoir's Hyper Voice; Toxtricity without Punk Rock drops from a guaranteed OHKO on Basculegion to 12.5%; meta Volcarona is a Bold support set with 0 SpA points; Vivillon without Compound Eyes has 75% Sleep Powder. Whether a Simple Beam line is worth it depends on move order, the threshold gained on the chosen targets, and whether a partner can do the job another way.

- **Blastoise-Mega (07):** plain +2 Water Spout already OHKOs Incineroar, Kingambit and Garchomp, and +2 Speed (260) already clears the unboosted top 15 (fastest is 214). Simple also removes Mega Launcher, and at -2 Def Rillaboom's Wood Hammer OHKOs (342-404%).
- **Kommo-o (09):** plain +1 already OHKOs Salamence-Mega, Garchomp and Kingambit. Simple replaces Soundproof, so Gardevoir-Mega's Hyper Voice now lands and OHKOs a +2 SpD Kommo-o at 2/3 HP (105-126%).
- **Toxtricity (10):** the receiver runs a Modest set, so +2 Atk does nothing, and losing Punk Rock drops Overdrive on Basculegion from a guaranteed OHKO to 12.5%. The gimmick is net negative.
- **Volcarona (14):** the meta Volcarona is a Bold, 0-SpA Rage Powder support. Its one flip (Kingambit, 84-99.5% to 113-133%) comes from reaching +2, which two plain Quiver Dances also reach.
- **Vivillon (15):** +0 Hurricane already OHKOs Rillaboom and Sneasler. Simple removes Compound Eyes, which drops Sleep Powder from 97.5% to 75% accuracy.

The other entries in this list fail on a mechanic or on the receiver's real set:

- **Sneasler + Oranguru Symbiosis (19):** the mechanic contradicts itself. Symbiosis hands Sneasler a new item, and Unburden's Speed doubling requires holding no item (`unburden.condition.onModifySpe` checks `!pokemon.item`). Speed stays 172.
- **Sceptile Swagger (26):** the receiver is a Timid special set, so +2 Atk does nothing. At +0 it already moves first against 12 of the top 15, and its meta Psychic Seed already doubles its Speed.
- **Indeedee-F Flatter (29):** Own Tempo replaces Psychic Surge, which is why Indeedee-F is played. Psychic in Psychic Terrain already OHKOs Sneasler. At +1 without terrain, Basculegion only moves from a 3HKO to a 69.5% 2HKO.
- **Golurk No Guard Dynamic Punch (doubles 27):** Iron Fist Headlong Rush OHKOs Kingambit; No Guard Dynamic Punch only 2HKOs it. In BSS (15), No Guard is already Golurk's most used ability.
- **Flail and Reversal on the wrong sets:** Gyarados in doubles (22) has no 1-HP Flail OHKO on its targets; Incineroar takes 59-70%. Mamoswine (23): non-STAB Flail at 1 HP is weaker than full-HP Icicle Crash (59-69% vs 149-177% on Salamence-Mega). Gyarados in BSS (11) is an Impish 1-Atk set: Garchomp takes 52-61%. Flareon (12) is a Careful 10-Atk set and only 2HKOs Garchomp. Slurpuff Gem Giga Impact (08) comes from a Calm 0-Atk set and does 31-37% to Garchomp.
- **Fast Reversal that is not fast:** Scizor in doubles (12) and BSS (05) OHKOs its targets at 1 HP, but at 85 Speed it moves first against only 4 of the top 15 doubles sets and 3 of the top 15 BSS sets.
- **Endeavor plus Sash:** in doubles (Persian-Alola 05, Raichu-Alola 06), 4 of the top 15 sets carry Fake Out (Rillaboom, Sneasler, Incineroar, Raichu-Mega-Y), and Fake Out breaks Sash. In BSS (Hawlucha 01, Salazzle 03), Gholdengo, Mimikyu and Aegislash are immune, Hippowdon's sand breaks Sash, and nobody is left to finish the 1-HP foe.
- **Hawlucha Gem Giga Impact (BSS 09):** it only 2HKOs Garchomp, Salamence-Mega and Primarina, then spends the doubled Speed on a recharge turn. Meta Hawlucha already gets x2 Speed from its Seed.

## Which E2 gimmicks survive

These are worth a test: Sneasler Swagger + Persim (doubles-03), Rhyperior Sash Reversal under Trick Room with Armor Tail cover (doubles-13), Arcanine Beat Up + Justified (doubles-17), and Choice Scarf Ninetales Memento (doubles-21). Machamp and Golurk No Guard Dynamic Punch are kept, but they are the standard sets, not discoveries.

The rest are deprioritized for the selected sets and targets. Eight of them (Healing Wish on Lopunny-Mega and Gardevoir-Mega in both formats, Memento on Glimmora-Mega in both formats and on Chandelure-Mega, Destiny Bond on Houndoom-Mega) spend the team's only Mega on a sacrifice. Gengar's Destiny Bond (178 Speed) moves after every sweeper worth trading with: Salamence-Mega 189, Raichu-Mega-Y 200, Basculegion 214, and seeded Sneasler 344. The two Arboliva Harvest Lum entries (doubles-16, singles-07) change no damage or Speed result.

## Method

For each gimmick, I named 2 to 4 target matchups, the losses it accepts, and one dealbreaker threat. Then I ran only the calculations that could change the decision. There is no matrix. All damage comes from the native Smogon Champions calculator through `workbench/tools/calculator.mjs` (damage-calc revision `c1eb09be483f0bbf65132bf3703ee677195132f3`, generation 0). The verdicts quote its `fullDesc` and `kochance` as returned. Speed is read from the calculator's returned stats and compared directly: stage multipliers, x2 for Unburden, x1.5 for Choice Scarf, and reversed order for Trick Room sets (minus-Speed nature with 0 Speed points). The Speed comparison is against the top 15 sets in each format.

The calculator refuses abilities a species cannot have, such as Simple. Simple Beam receivers were therefore calculated with doubled boosts on a legal ability. Simple itself has no damage effect, but the lost ability does. Blastoise-Mega still carries Mega Launcher in the calculator, which does not touch Water Spout. Toxtricity was calculated with Technician, which does not touch an 80 BP Overdrive, to stand in for losing Punk Rock. Kommo-o was calculated with Bulletproof, to stand in for losing Soundproof.

## Numbers observed

Sets come from `data/meta-sets.json` candidates. These are combinations of the most common choice in each usage marginal (spread, ability, item, moves). They are constructed sets, not observed full sets, and carry no joint probability. 33 of the 45 receiver sets fall outside that file's top 60; for those I built the same kind of set from the usage files' marginals, and `outcomes.json` records which source each receiver used.
