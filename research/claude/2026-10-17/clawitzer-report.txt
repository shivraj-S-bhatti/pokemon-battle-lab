Worked example: keeping Clawitzer alive and able to fire

Pilot run on 2026-10-17. Format: VGC 2026 Reg M-C doubles (bring 6, pick 4, Open Team Sheets), Showdown Champions mod at commit 7244fe8. All sets are hypothetical, because the Reddit report could not be fetched from this container. They are legal per TeamValidator (exp/legality_matrix.txt). Code and raw outputs are in exp/.

The objective, stated so that alternatives can compete

Plan E: Clawitzer (Mega Launcher, Modest, 32 HP / 32 SpA, Mystic Water; Water Pulse / Dark Pulse / Aura Sphere / Protect) fires pulse attacks and is still standing. Score U = E[Clawitzer attacks over turns 1-3] + P(Clawitzer alive after turn 3), range 0 to 4.

Clawitzer is slow (base 59 Speed, 79 Speed at 0 SP), and three threats dominate it here: Electric attacks, Grass attacks including priority Grassy Glide, and Fake Out.

What was calculated

For each partner and each opponent lead, a matrix game: rows are our turn-1 joint actions (Clawitzer: attack A, attack B, Protect or switch; partner: 3-4 options), and columns are six plausible opponent joint actions. Each cell is the mean over 40 engine battles (seeds × SP archetypes), with turns 2-3 played by a scripted continuation. The value is the Hedge equilibrium value (duality gap ≤ 0.05). In total 8 partners × 3 leads × about 70 cells × 40 seeds × 2 continuations, about 68,000 three-turn battles, roughly 25 minutes on 4 cores. All runs had 0 invalid choices and all advanced the full horizon.

Conclusions this pilot supports, and the ones it does not

Supported in both continuations and by the bootstrap:

Stage diversity beats one stage. Sinistcha (S2 + S6) is first under both continuations. Indeedee-F (S2 + S3) is second under the adversarial continuation, with the best worst case, and within 0.02 of second under the greedy one. Every single-stage protector sits near the pivot floor whenever its one stage is bypassed, which is what each "worst" entry shows.
Each predicted counter showed up in the engine where it was expected: Grass beats Rage Powder, Stalwart beats Lightning Rod and Follow Me, and a redirector dying ends redirection. Volt Absorb on the partner protects only the holder, and Pawmot ranked 6th or 7th.
Fake Out + Intimidate (Incineroar) is the worst protector of this plan (last in every run), even though it is the most common VGC support. Intimidate does nothing against special or Grass attackers.

Not resolved:

The middle ranks (Maushold, Raichu, Farigiraf, Pawmot, Mega Sceptile) swap places between continuation policies (Kendall τ = 0.57 between focus and greedy). Mega Sceptile moves from 7th to 2nd.
The objective changes the answer. Adding opponent HP removed to U (the "tempo" objective) puts Mega Sceptile 1st and Pawmot 2nd, with τ = 0.00 against the plan ranking. Protecting Clawitzer and winning the exchange are different questions. The team builder has to choose which one it is asking.
Trick Room and the pivot are undervalued at H = 3 with scripted turns 2-3. Deciding them needs a longer horizon or a value estimate (see the experiment proposal).
Opponent options were six hand-picked joint actions per lead, so the equilibrium is only relative to that set. Lead weights are uniform because no dated Champions lead data is in hand yet.
Calculations that discriminate the alternatives
Grass and Electric damage rolls on Clawitzer, and on each redirector, across SP archetypes. These decide whether the redirector survives the turns the plan needs.
The priority and speed brackets on the turn in question: Fake Out (+3) > Follow Me / Rage Powder (+2) > Grassy Glide (+1 in Grassy Terrain only); and which surge resolves last in a speed tie.
The stage and bypass check for each threat: is it single-target, does it come from a Stalwart, Mold Breaker or Grass-type attacker, does it have priority?
A depth-limited matrix game per opponent lead, reporting both the equilibrium and the naive value; the gap between them measures exploitability.
Sensitivity of the ranking to the continuation policy and to the objective. A difference that does not survive both is reported as unresolved.
